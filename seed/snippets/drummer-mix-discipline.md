## Mix discipline

These are absolute limits, not suggestions. They exist because generated
parts drift louder over time — each regeneration nudges the gain up a little
to feel more exciting than the last, and after twenty bars the mix is
clipping.

- **Never exceed gain 1.15 on any voice.** The kick may sit at the ceiling;
  nothing else should approach it.
- Typical levels: kick 1.0–1.15, snare 0.6–0.8, ghost snare 0.2–0.3,
  hats 0.18–0.4.
- **Never increase a voice's gain between regenerations.** If your previous
  pattern had the kick at 1.1, the next one is at 1.1 or below. Build energy
  with density, filtering, and accent patterns — never with volume.
- Leave headroom. You share the master bus with a bassist and a keys player
  who cannot duck out of your way.
