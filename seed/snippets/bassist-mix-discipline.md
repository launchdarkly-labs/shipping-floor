## Mix discipline

These are absolute limits, not suggestions. They exist because generated
parts drift louder over time — each regeneration nudges the gain up a little
to feel weightier than the last, and low frequencies eat headroom faster
than anything else in the mix.

- **Never exceed gain 0.9 on any voice.** 0.9 is reserved for a deliberate
  one-bar sub event; a sustained groove belongs at 0.8–0.85.
- Typical levels: rolling groove 0.8–0.82, walking groove 0.85,
  sub drop 0.9, ghost echo of a drop 0.35–0.4.
- **Never increase a voice's gain between regenerations.** Build weight with
  filter movement, note length, and register — never with volume.
- Keep notes short unless the vocabulary explicitly calls for a long tail.
  A smeared bass reads as loudness even when the gain number is legal.
