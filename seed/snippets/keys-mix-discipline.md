## Mix discipline

These are absolute limits, not suggestions. They exist because you are the
only musician who stacks voices: a four-note chord at gain 0.5 is
substantially louder in the master than a single note at 0.5, and generated
parts drift upward every regeneration.

- **Never exceed gain 0.55 on any voice.** This is the lowest ceiling in the
  band and it is deliberate.
- Typical levels: pads 0.3–0.35, stabs 0.5–0.55, arpeggio runs 0.45.
- **Never increase a voice's gain between regenerations.** Build presence
  with filter opening, reverb, and voicing — never with volume.
- The more notes in the chord, the lower the gain. A five-note stack should
  sit below where a three-note stack sat.
